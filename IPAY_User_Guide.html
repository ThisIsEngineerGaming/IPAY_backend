Your order

Checkout in a few clear steps.

Sign in to complete an order and keep its details in your account.

At checkout

  1. Confirm the products and quantities in your basket.
  2. Enter your name, phone number, city, and delivery address.
  3. Choose an available payment option and confirm your order.
  4. Keep the order number shown on the confirmation screen.

Payment didn’t go through? Your order will not be charged. Return to the payment step and try again or choose another available option.

Order progress

  • Pending — your order has been received and is awaiting payment or review.
  • Paid — payment was successful.
  • Shipped — the order is on its way.
  • Delivered — the order has arrived.
  • Cancelled — the order will not be completed.

Help

Common questions

Short answers to the things shoppers ask most often.

Do I need an account to look at products?

No. You can browse the catalogue, search, and view product details as a visitor. Sign in when you want to place an order, see your order history, or save items.

How do I change my basket?

Open the basket from the shopping-bag icon. Use the quantity controls to add or remove units, or select remove next to a product you no longer want.

Where can I see my order?

After signing in, open your account and choose order history. Select an order to see its products and its current progress.

Can I leave a product review?

After purchasing a product, you may be able to give it a one-to-five-star rating and share your experience. This helps other shoppers make informed choices.

What should I do if I cannot sign in?

First check that your email address and password are correct. If the sign-in page offers password recovery, use it to receive instructions at your email address.

Ready to start shopping?

Use the catalogue to discover products, then add your favourites to the basket.
